## 1. Dominating Playback Controls

The DVR Playback function gives you mastery over both live and recorded television. Once you start recording a show or pause a live broadcast, the DVR begins saving the stream to its hard drive, allowing you to manipulate the viewing timeline.

### Playback Button Functions

The primary buttons on your remote control govern the playback experience:

| Button Name | Icon | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Play** | ▶ | Starts playback of a recording or resumes content after a pause. |
| **Pause** | ⏸ | Freezes the video and temporarily stops the playback stream. |
| **Stop** | ◼ | Ends the playback session and returns you to the main menu or live TV. |
| **Fast-Forward** | ⏩ | Jumps forward in the content timeline, typically at 2x, 4x, or 8x speed. |
| **Rewind** | ⏪ | Moves backward in the content timeline to review previous scenes. |

### Performing Playback Actions (Task)

To manage a recording from your library:

1.  Access your stored content by pressing the **Recordings** or **Library** button on your remote.
2.  Navigate to the title you wish to watch and press **OK** or **Select**.
3.  To begin watching, press **Play** (▶).
4.  If you need to briefly interrupt viewing, press **Pause** (⏸).
5.  To skip forward through a section (e.g., commercials), repeatedly press **Fast-Forward** (⏩) until you reach the desired point. Press **Play** (▶) to resume normal speed.
