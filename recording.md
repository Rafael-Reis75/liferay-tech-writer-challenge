## 2. How to Schedule a Recording

Scheduling a program ensures that the DVR automatically captures your favorite shows, even when you are not home. This process utilizes the Electronic Program Guide (EPG) to identify upcoming broadcast times.

### Scheduling a One-Time Recording (Task)

Follow these steps to schedule a single episode or program for recording:

1.  Press the **Guide** button on your remote control to open the Electronic Program Guide (EPG).
2.  Use the directional arrows to navigate through the channels and time slots to find the specific program you want to record.
3.  Highlight the program and press the **OK** or **Select** button to view the program details screen.
4.  On the details screen, select the **Record** button or option.
5.  Confirm the recording. The program guide will now display a red circle icon (⏺) next to the program title, indicating that the recording has been successfully scheduled.

> **Note:** Ensure your DVR has an active connection to the internet and power source. Loss of connection can prevent the EPG from updating, potentially causing a scheduled recording to fail.

### Advanced Recording Tip: Series Recordings

If you intend to record all future episodes of a program, look for the **Record Series** or **Create Recurring Recording** option on the program details screen. Selecting this option tells the DVR to automatically track and record the program every time it airs.
