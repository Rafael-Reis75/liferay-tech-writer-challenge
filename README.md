# Digital Video Recorder (DVR) User Guide: Essential Functions

The Digital Video Recorder (DVR) allows you to pause live television, skip commercials, and record your favorite shows, giving you complete control over your viewing experience.

This guide provides a foundational understanding of your DVR's core capabilities. By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to manage recorded content and schedule new recordings effortlessly.

## Covered Sections

* **1. Dominating Playback Controls:** Learn how to start, stop, pause, and navigate through recorded and live-paused content using your remote control.
* **2. How to Schedule a Recording:** Step-by-step instructions on using the Electronic Program Guide (EPG) to schedule one-time or recurring recordings for TV shows.
